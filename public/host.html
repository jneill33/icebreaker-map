    How this works
    1. Guests scan the QR code (or open the link) on their phones.
    2. They enter a name, an answer and a US ZIP code. No app or account.
    3. A pin appears here instantly. Click any pin to read the answer.
    4. Pins are rounded to city level, and ZIP codes are never saved.
    5. Ending the session deletes everything. It also clears itself after 12 hours.
