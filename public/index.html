How it works

Three steps, zero setup

You run the session from this screen. Your group only needs a phone.

1

Pick a question

Choose a suggestion or write your own. It's the one thing everyone answers when they join.

2

Show the QR code

Put it on the projector or share the link. Guests scan it with their phone camera, then enter a name, an answer and their ZIP code.

3

Watch the map fill up

Each person appears as a pin the moment they join. Click a pin to see their name and answer, and use it to start conversations.

Privacy built in

Friendly to the room, careful with data

📍

City-level pins

Locations are rounded to about 7 miles, so nobody's exact position is ever shown.

🔒

ZIPs aren't saved

A ZIP code is used once to place the pin, then thrown away.

🙋

Guests stay in control

Anyone can remove their own pin and answer at any time.

🧹

Auto-cleanup

End the session to delete everything, or it clears itself after 12 hours.
